About

"Cafe Timer" is an unofficial fan-made tool that supports the Cafe feature of the game Blue Archive.
It notifies you when students can be visited at the cafe and helps Sensei keep track of their routine.

Features

  • Countdown until students can be tapped again
  • Countdown to student visit times (4:00 / 16:00 JST)
  • Cooldown management for invitation tickets
  • Calendar view of tap history
  • Bond rank records and graph

Supported platforms

Works on the latest browsers on PC and smartphones.
As a PWA, it can be added to your home screen and used like an app.

User Guide

Using Cafe Timer

  • Next Tap: Counts down the time until you can interact with students again. Press "Tap" to record it and start the countdown.
  • Next Call: Manages the cooldown of invitation tickets. Press the button at the time you use a ticket to start the countdown to the next available time.

Tap history

  • SAVE IMAGE: Saves the tap history of a month as an image, handy for sharing on social media.

Display settings

  • Use the sun/moon button in the header to switch between light and dark mode.
  • In Settings you can choose Auto (follows your device), Light or Dark, change the accent color, and pick a language.
  • Times are shown in your device's time zone. You can change it in Settings.

History and sync

When you sign in with a Google account, your tap history and other records are saved to your own Google Drive. Sign in with the same account on another device to share and sync the same data.

About notifications

From "Notifications" in Settings, you can get push notifications when students can be tapped again. A notification arrives 3 hours after you record a tap. If the next 4:00/16:00 JST comes first, none is scheduled because you can interact at that time anyway. No notification is sent for 4:00/16:00 JST itself, since those times are fixed.

Up to 10 devices can be registered per user. When the limit is exceeded, the oldest registrations are removed first. Turn notifications off from the Settings of devices you no longer use.

If notifications do not work

If you use an ad blocker (content blocker) such as AdGuard or uBlock Origin, the notification system may be mistaken for tracking and the permission dialog may not appear.

When setting up notifications, add this site (domain) to the allow list or temporarily turn the blocker off, then reload the page and set up notifications again.

FAQ

Features

Q: I tapped by mistake. Can I delete the record?
A: Individual tap records cannot be edited or deleted. Bond rank records can be deleted for the latest 6 entries.
Q: Are additional invitation tickets supported?
A: Not at the moment.
Q: Is a tap in the game reflected automatically?
A: This site does not access any in-game data. After tapping in the in-game cafe, please press the Tap button here manually as well.
Q: Can I export the tap history?
A: Export as text data is not supported at the moment. To share on social media, use the monthly image export.
Q: Can I carry over data from the previous (Discord login) version?
A: Yes. Import the data (JSON) exported from the old version via "Import old data" in Settings. Please sign in with your Google account first.
Q: The history calendar doesn't match the dates I actually tapped.
A: The history calendar treats the period from 4:00 AM to 3:59 AM the following day (Japan Standard Time) as one day. Times shown elsewhere in the app use your local time zone (you can change it in Settings), but the calendar's day boundaries follow the game's reset time, so the dates may differ from your local dates if you live in a time zone other than UTC+9.

System

Q: Can I use it offline?
A: No. An internet connection is required.
Q: Does it use data inside the game?
A: This site does not use any features implemented in Blue Archive and does not access game data, so it cannot affect your in-game account.
Q: Are timers and tickets kept after I close the app?
A: Yes. Times are stored in your Google Drive, so the latest state is reflected even after closing the browser or accessing from another device.
Q: Where is my data stored?
A: In a hidden folder dedicated to this app in your own Google Drive. Tap history and similar data are not stored on the operator's servers. The app only accesses its dedicated folder and never reads other files in your Drive.
Q: Do I need to back up my data?
A: Data is stored in the Drive of the Google account you sign in with, so it is not lost when you sign out. However, permanent storage is not guaranteed, so we recommend keeping your own records.
Q: Can I use it on several devices at the same time?
A: You can use multiple devices, but operating them at the same time may cause data inconsistencies. Reload the other device before you start operating it.
Q: Can I manage multiple accounts on one device?
A: Yes, but the site has no account switching, so you need to sign out and sign in each time. Notifications are managed per device, and only one account per device can receive them.
Q: Is this service free?
A: Yes, all features are free. Since this is a personal project, specifications may change or the service may stop without notice.
Q: Notifications do not arrive.
A: Check that notifications are "allowed" in your browser settings, and try setting up notifications again (see also the User Guide). We are unable to handle missed notifications caused by your device. As stated in the Terms of Service, notification delivery is not guaranteed.
Q: "Drive authorization has expired" is displayed.
A: For security, access to Google Drive expires after about an hour. It is normally renewed automatically; if it cannot be, press the "Re-authenticate" button at the top of the screen.
Q: Are there other sign-in methods besides Google?
A: Only Google sign-in is supported at present. The site is not designed for switching accounts, so there are no plans to add other authentication systems.
